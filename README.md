# Watchlog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Watchlog is an anime tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect any data about you.

## What stays on your device

Your list (titles, status, progress, scores and notes) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you export a backup, the file goes only where you choose to share it.

## What is sent to third parties

To show anime information, Watchlog contacts these services over HTTPS:

- **AniList** (anilist.co): your search text, and the identifiers of titles you browse, open or track, so it can return details, artwork and airing times.
- **Jikan** (jikan.moe): the identifier of a title you open, to look up where it streams.

These services receive ordinary network request data (such as your IP address) as with any web request, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Watchlog sends them no name, email, device identifier or other personal information.

## Notifications

If you turn on airing alerts, Watchlog schedules notifications locally on your device. No push server is involved.

## Children

Watchlog does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
